PPR pipes, explained simply

Never bought plumbing pipes before? Here is everything you need to choose the right ones in two minutes.

The basics

What is PPR?

A plastic pipe for water in homes. It is light, never rusts, and the pieces are joined by heating them with a small welding iron: no glue and no threads. That is why it is the most common choice for new water lines.

Hot or cold water? Look at «PN»

PN shows how much pressure the pipe can take: the higher the number, the thicker the wall. PN20 is the safe choice for both hot and cold water and for heating. Thinner pipes like PN10 are for cold water only. Pipes with a glass-fibre layer expand less when hot.

Which size do I need?

The size is the outer width in millimeters, written with the sign Ø. For one tap, a toilet or a washing machine take Ø20. For the main line of a whole apartment or small house take Ø32. Bigger sizes are for large houses and heating. The table below shows each size.

How much do I buy?

Pipes are sold in 4 meter lengths. Measure the route from the water source to the tap and add about 10% for cuts, or let the calculator below do it. You will also need fittings (elbows, tees, couplings): call us and we will count them for you.
